About

About PIC 2027

Physics in Collisions updates the key topics in particle physics where new results have been published in the past year, or are expected before the symposium. Talks are designed to start informal discussion of new experimental results and what they imply.

The program covers experimental particle physics through to astroparticle physics, with invited plenary talks and contributed talks.

The 46th symposium is hosted in Melbourne at Monash College's Docklands campus, a short walk from Southern Cross Station.

  • 02Electroweak physics

Travel

Arriving in Melbourne

From Melbourne Airport

The SkyBus express runs from Melbourne Airport (MEL) to Southern Cross Station, about 300 m from the venue. Taxis and rideshare take around 30 minutes outside peak hours.

By train

Take any City Loop train to Southern Cross Station, then walk west along Collins Street. Regional V/Line services also arrive at Southern Cross.

By tram

Routes 11 and 48 run along Collins Street from the north and east of the city and stop right outside the building.

By bus

Routes 232, 235 and 237 stop directly in front of the building.

Accommodation is not included in the registration fee. The venue is well served by trams and trains, so most of the city centre is within easy reach.

Sponsorship

Sponsorship helps keep registration affordable, especially for students and early-career researchers, and supports the conference's social program. We welcome support from industry, research institutions and funding bodies.

Why sponsor

Reach the particle physics community

An international audience
